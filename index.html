Dramaturg's notes

One forest. Many voices.
Everything at stake.

FABULA brings the climate crisis and the threats facing endemic wildlife to a musical stage in constant transformation—from a circle rooted in tradition to a noisy modern world.

Documentation frame · Origins

ORIGIN

The concept grew from concern about the climate crisis—especially forest fires and the threat of extinction facing Indonesia's endemic wildlife. The work brings environmental issues to a dynamic stage through clashing musical characters who represent a divided society. A circular 360-degree stage was conceived to give the audience a fresh experience and a broader perspective.

Documentation frame · The alliance

PREMISE

Endangered animals unite against the destruction of their habitat by forest fires. But betrayal from within the alliance leaves them trapped and at the mercy of the illegal wildlife trade.

Documentation frame · Conflict

CONFLICT

After forest fires destroy their home, rare animals unite against the humans destroying their habitat. Their struggle takes a turn when one of them betrays the others. The entire alliance is captured, caged, and sold in an illegal animal market—a dark ending underscored by the clash of traditional ethnic music and modern phonk beats.

Documentation frame · Themes

THEME

The destruction of one habitat threatens every living thing, not just a single species. Unity is the animals' greatest strength, yet greed and betrayal can break it from within. The shift from traditional music to modern sounds reflects how nature, once alive and deeply rooted in culture, is now reduced to a commodity.

Documentation frame · The stakes

STAKES

The future of Indonesia's wildlife hangs in the balance. If the animals fail to unite, habitats will keep disappearing, and more species will face extinction and illegal trade. More than their survival, Indonesia's natural heritage is at risk. The central question remains: if unity cannot overcome greed and betrayal, what will be left of the world they are fighting for?

Documentation frame · The experience

WHY SEE IT AGAIN?

The show invites repeat viewing through the animals' resistance to failed wildfire responses, private companies, and greedy individuals. Its transformation from a 180° to a 360° stage, paired with a musical journey from traditional sounds to phonk, makes every viewing vivid and engaging.
